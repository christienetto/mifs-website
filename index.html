How it works

Three taps to the
good part.

1

Pick a song.

Search by title, artist, or even a line you remember. Type “send me the” and MIFS finds the song by its lyrics. Full songs come from the MIFS Library, with Apple Music charts and your own audio right beside them.

2

Find the moment.

Drag the waveform, or just tap a lyric. The selection lands on that line, and the lyrics inside it light up. Choose 5, 10 or 15 seconds, then preview it before anyone else hears it.

3

Send it.

It arrives as an interactive bubble, with the artwork, the real waveform of your moment, and a play button. One tap and it plays, lyrics and all. No links to open, no app to switch to.

    Small clips.
    Big details.

    Lyrics that pick
    the moment.

    Every line is synced to the song. Tap one and your snippet starts right there, a quarter second early, so the first word never gets clipped.

    Plays anywhere.

    Every moment you send becomes a mif: a short clip with its own link. On a phone without MIFS, in any browser, in any messenger with link previews, it just plays.

    Your audio stays yours.

    Every snippet, saved.

    The Snippets tab keeps everything you’ve made, so the perfect ten seconds are always one tap from being sent again.

    Songs on demand

    The house band

    Every note, drawn in code.

    MIFS ships with three original songs to try it with. The chords, melodies, drums, vocals, lyrics and artwork were all generated from scratch, then released into the public domain. Tap a cover to hear its chorus.

    Under the hood

    A music server
    built for moments.

    Behind MIFS is a small, fast Go service that stores full songs, precomputes their waveforms, serves synced lyrics, and turns any moment into a clip that plays on any device.

    1

    static binary

    Pure-Go SQLite with no cgo and no runtime dependencies. It cross-compiles for any Linux host.

    256kbps

    AAC, streamed

    The index sits at the front of every file, so playback seeks straight to your moment with range requests. The app never downloads a whole song to preview ten seconds.

    10/s

    waveform points

    Computed once at import, with the same analysis the app runs on the phone, so the editor draws the whole song immediately.

    FTS5

    lyric search

    A ranked full-text index over titles, artists, albums and every lyric line. Remember a line, find the song.

    SHA-256

    addressed media

    Audio and artwork are named by their hash and never change in place. They cache forever and move to any CDN untouched.

    304

    cheap revalidation

    Weak ETags on every API response keep the app’s cache fresh with near-empty round trips, so new songs show up instantly.

    Tech Specs

    Works with
    • iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later
    • Messages, as an iMessage app
    • Telegram, as a Mini App on every platform
    • Any web browser, through mif share links
    Snippets
    • 5, 10 or 15 seconds
    • From anywhere in a full song
    • Chosen by waveform or by lyric line
    • Previewed before sending
    Song sources
    • MIFS Library: full songs with synced lyrics
    • Songs on demand, discovered through Spotify and Deezer
    • Apple Music: search and charts, from 30-second previews
    • Your own files and DRM-free library songs
    Lyrics
    • Line-synced, with an explicit end for every line
    • Fetched automatically from LRCLIB for new songs
    • Searchable, so a remembered line finds the song
    Sharing
    • Interactive iMessage bubble with artwork and waveform
    • Telegram card with a Play Snippet button
    • Clips of your own audio sent as native audio messages
    • A share link for every mif, with rich previews
    Server
    • Go, SQLite with FTS5, and ffmpeg
    • AAC 256 kbps with fast start and range requests
    • Content-addressed media, ready for object storage and a CDN
    Privacy
    • Clips of audio you own never reach MIFS servers
    • Apple Music previews are never re-hosted
    • Telegram snippets are encoded in the link, so nothing is stored
