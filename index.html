A song is three minutes long. The part you want to send is ten seconds. MIFS finds it, clips it, and drops it in the chat, where it plays with a tap.

How it works

Three taps to the
good part.

1

Pick a song.

Search by title or artist across Spotify and Apple Music. Every song arrives with its artwork, and with synced lyrics whenever they exist.

2

Find the moment.

Drag the waveform, or just tap a lyric. The selection lands on that line, and the lyrics inside it light up. Choose 5, 10 or 15 seconds, then preview it before anyone else hears it.

3

Send it.

It arrives as an interactive bubble, with the artwork, the real waveform of your moment, and a play button. One tap and it plays, lyrics and all. No links to open, no app to switch to.
